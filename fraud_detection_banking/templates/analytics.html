Why Accuracy is Misleading in Banking Fraud Detection

In banking datasets, fraudulent transactions typically make up only 1% to 15% of all transactions. A naive dummy model predicting "0 (Legitimate)" on all transactions would achieve 85% to 99% accuracy while missing 100% of the fraud cases, leading to catastrophic financial loss.

  • Recall (Sensitivity): Dictates what percentage of actual fraud is intercepted. High recall ensures the bank does not let fraudsters escape.
  • Precision: Measures what percentage of flagged transactions were genuinely fraudulent. High precision prevents humiliating legitimate cardholders with false card declines.
  • ROC-AUC: Measures discriminatory power across all classification thresholds regardless of class imbalance.
